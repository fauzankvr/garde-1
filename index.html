How it works

One platform to GARDE them all

Today’s email threats are more complex than they have ever been. AI is supercharging attack speed, scale, and sophistication. Garde brings together advanced security features designed to protect enterprises across the entire email security stack.

Reputation Layer

Garde filters emails from suspicious senders, keeping known malicious actors outside your organisation’s inboxes.

Sender & IP Scoring Pre-ingress
LAYER 02

Identity Layer

Garde uses multiple authentication checks to verify sender identities before messages reach your inbox.

SPF · DKIM · DMARC · ARC Authenticated
