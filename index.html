Key features

Every layer of email defense, in one platform

Garde inspects authentication, reputation, content, and payloads on every message so threats never reach a mailbox.

  • Multi-Layer Email Protection

    Combine reputation analysis, sender authentication, content inspection, and machine learning to stop advanced email threats before delivery.

  • Anti-Phishing Protection

    Detect phishing attempts, spoofed domains, impersonation attacks, and malicious URLs using advanced threat intelligence and behavioral analysis.

  • Spam Filtering

    Intelligent spam detection using statistical analysis, pattern recognition, and adaptive filtering to reduce unwanted email.

  • Malware & Attachment Scanning

    Scan attachments and email content for malware, ransomware, viruses, and suspicious payloads before they reach users.

  • Sender Authentication

    SPF validation, DKIM verification, DMARC enforcement, and ARC validation on every inbound message.

  • Reputation-Based Filtering

    Score sender, IP, domain, and URL reputation continuously to weight verdicts with real-world signal.

  • AI-Powered Threat Detection

    Machine learning and behavioral analysis identify new phishing campaigns, fraud attempts, and emerging threats.

  • Real-Time Threat Intelligence

    Continuously updated threat feeds and security intelligence databases deliver proactive protection.

  • Policy Enforcement

    Rate limiting, greylisting, custom security policies, allow lists, and block lists under one control plane.

  • Email Content Analysis

    Inspect headers, MIME structure, URLs, attachments, HTML content, and embedded links for hidden intent.

How it works

Seven checks between the internet and your inbox

  1. 1

    Incoming Email Received

    Messages arrive at the Garde MX or inline gateway.

  2. 2

    Authentication Checks

    SPF, DKIM, DMARC, and ARC results are resolved and aligned.

  3. 3

    Content & Reputation Analysis

    Headers, MIME parts, and sender reputation are scored.

  4. 4

    AI Threat Detection

    Machine-learning models flag phishing and fraud patterns.

  5. 5

    Malware & URL Scanning

    Attachments and links are scanned and detonated as needed.

  6. 6

    Threat Classification & Action

    Verdicts map to policy: tag, quarantine, or reject.

  7. 7

    Secure Delivery or Block

    Clean mail is delivered; threats are blocked and logged.
